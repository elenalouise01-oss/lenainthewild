# Naluri Socials AI: positioning brief (from Lena, 23 Nov 2026)

Read this before writing any Naluri Socials AI website copy, landing pages or marketing.
Ask Lena for her own words; don't invent language that doesn't sound like her.

## What it is
Eight years of professional social media expertise (social media specialist, digital
marketing, client work, strategies and content workflows) turned into personalised
AI-powered systems built with custom Claude skills.

Lena grew her own wellness business to 13K Instagram and 18.8K TikTok followers while
working as a PT, and knows firsthand what consistently creating and growing a presence takes.

**AI is the vessel. Expertise is the foundation. Freedom is the outcome.**
Not an AI business that happens to do social media: an experienced specialist who has
automated her own processes and knowledge.

## What the system covers
Social media strategy · content strategy and planning · content ideas · reel editing ·
carousel creation and editing · content writing and captions · automated DM responses ·
content scheduling. Custom Claude skills built from real industry experience, not
generic tools or prompt packs.

## How the service works
Lena builds and implements a personalised system for each client, set up for their
business, brand identity, visual branding, tone of voice, audience and content strategy.
She hands it over for the client to run themselves, with optional ongoing support
(adjustments, troubleshooting, extra help).
NOT traditional social media management: she doesn't take over accounts or create
their content indefinitely.

## Philosophy
Building your dream business without accidentally creating another job for yourself.
Lena built her wellness business while working as a PT, creating content constantly,
growing her audience and eventually burning out from doing everything manually. AI
removes the repetitive production work so there's more time and capacity for what
actually moves a business forward.

## Relationship with Lena in the Wild
- Lena in the Wild: creating your dream life (freedom, lifestyle, travel, self-discovery).
- Naluri Socials AI: building your dream business (business ownership, social media
  systems, AI automation, a business that supports the life you want).
- Shared belief: your business should support your life, not consume it.

## Voice
Conversational, direct, honest, thoughtful, confident without being salesy, personal
without oversharing, focused on possibilities rather than manufactured pain points.

Avoid: generic marketing language, AI buzzwords, corporate or agency terms, dramatic
pain-point marketing, empty promises about effortless growth, making AI the whole value.
Don't manufacture a brand personality: her expertise, experience and perspective are
the differentiators.

## Core line to remember
Naluri Socials AI takes eight years of social media expertise and transforms it into
personalised AI-powered systems that automate the content creation workflow for
business owners. AI is how the expertise is delivered; the system is what the client
receives; time, freedom and capacity are the outcome.
